What changed for 2026?

Both wings have movable elements. The car is narrower, tyres are slimmer, and the underside has a flatter floor instead of the previous generation’s deep Venturi tunnels. The rear beam wing is removed.

Active Aero and electrical Overtake Mode are separate systems. This explorer demonstrates Active Aero; it does not simulate energy deployment.

How does the airflow work?

Wings turn air and create pressure differences that push the car down. The floor still contributes downforce, while the diffuser manages the expansion of underbody flow. Behind the tyres and wings, disturbed air forms a wake.

These paths are animated illustrations, not CFD. Real flow depends on geometry, ride height, yaw and many other variables.
